ABOUT

I am an aspiring SOC Analyst L1 with training in threat detection, incident response, SIEM monitoring, and log analysis. I analyze Windows and Linux logs to find suspicious activity and understand what happened during a security event.

I perform alert triage - separating real threats from false positives quickly and accurately. I identify common attacks like phishing, brute force, password spraying, and lateral movement. I understand both host logs (Windows Event Logs, Linux syslog, EDR alerts) and network logs (firewall, IDS/IPS, proxy, VPN), and I correlate data from multiple sources to build a clear picture of an incident.

I follow frameworks like Cyber Kill Chain, MITRE ATT&CK, and Pyramid of Pain to think like an attacker and defend like an analyst. I document every finding clearly because "If it's not documented, it didn't happen." I escalate confirmed threats to L2/IR teams with proper evidence and context. I am passionate about blue team defense and committed to continuous learning and growth.
